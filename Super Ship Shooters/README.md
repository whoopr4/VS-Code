# Super Ship Shooters

Couch co-op side-scrolling space shooter for up to 8 players (gamepads, keyboard or touch), built with Three.js.

- **Play:** https://whoopr4.github.io/VS-Code/Super%20Ship%20Shooters/
- **Files:** `index.html` is the game. `audio/` holds the music — keep the two together, or the game falls back to its own built-in music.
- **Controls:** WASD to fly, Space to fire, F / right mouse / Alt for special weapons, E to link ships, Shift to dodge. On a gamepad: left stick, A to join, right stick to aim when linked. Press C (or Back) for the full controls screen.
- **Music:** "Neon Circuitry" (stage 1) and "Neon Circuitry Level 2" (stage 2) during waves; "Boss Battle Theme" and "Boss Music 2" for bosses; "Bee Boss Music" whenever the Wasp shows up.
