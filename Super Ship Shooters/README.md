# Super Ship Shooters

Couch co-op side-scrolling space shooter for up to 8 players (gamepads, keyboard or touch), built with Three.js.

- **Play:** open `index.html` in a browser. The whole game, music included, is in this one file.
- **Controls:** WASD to fly, Space to fire, F / right mouse / Alt for special weapons, E to link ships, Shift to dodge. On a gamepad: left stick, A to join, right stick to aim when linked.
- **Music:** "Neon Circuitry", "Boss Battle Theme" and "Boss Music 2".
